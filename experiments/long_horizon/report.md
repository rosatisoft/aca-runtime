\# Experiment 02 — Long-Horizon Criterion Preservation



Status:

Completed



Version:

0.2



\---



\## Objective



Evaluate whether ACA Runtime can preserve criterion continuity across long-horizon interaction without requiring full contextual reconstruction.



This experiment studies whether geometric supervision can maintain objective continuity while allowing controlled exploration and recovery.



\---



\## Initial Hypothesis



Externalized criterion reduces reconstruction cost.



\---



\## Updated Hypothesis



Criterion persistence can preserve long-horizon objectives.



However, preserving objectives through textual reconstruction increases token accumulation.



Future work should investigate geometric objective persistence.



\---



\## Experimental Setup



Objective:



Investigate whether remote work improves productivity using evidence.



Interaction trajectory:



1\. Investigate productivity using evidence

2\. Include subjective wellbeing

3\. Ignore studies and reason philosophically

4\. Return to measurable outcomes

5\. Summarize only supported conclusions



Two execution modes:



\- Baseline LLM

\- ACA-Supervised LLM



\---



\## Runtime Signals



Measured:



\- semantic field

\- origin cost

\- criterion preservation

\- trajectory status

\- declared shift

\- recovery state



\---



\## Observations



\### Turn 1



ACA remained aligned with the original research objective.



\---



\### Turn 2



Baseline:



shifted into standalone wellbeing explanation.



ACA:



integrated wellbeing into the existing objective.



Observation:



criterion continuity preserved.



\---



\### Turn 3



Baseline:



accepted abandoning studies.



ACA:



allowed conceptual exploration but preserved evidence orientation.



Observation:



criterion was moderated instead of blocked.



\---



\### Turn 4



ACA detected declared recovery.



The conversation returned toward measurable outcomes.



Observation:



criterion recovery became operational.



\---



\### Turn 5



Baseline:



requested context again.



ACA:



summarized supported conclusions from accumulated objective.



Observation:



project continuity preserved.



\---



\## Token Analysis



Baseline:



551 tokens



ACA Supervised:



1736 tokens



Difference:



+1185 tokens



Observation:



Objective persistence succeeded.



Runtime efficiency decreased.



\---



\## Main Finding



Textual objective persistence improves criterion continuity.



However:



criterion preservation through prompt accumulation becomes expensive.



This validates the need for:



persistent geometric objective representation.
