## Finding — Criterion Context Levels

Experimental observation suggests that ACA operates under two distinct criterion regimes.

### Conversation Mode

Criterion is reconstructed dynamically from semantic trajectory.

The system preserves local coherence through:
- semantic continuity
- orientation recovery
- trajectory supervision

No persistent objective declaration is required.

Suitable for:

- moderation
- conversational supervision
- local criterion recovery
- short-horizon reasoning

Observed behavior:

- drift detection
- declared recovery
- criterion re-anchoring
- narrative stabilization through measurable constraints


### Project Mode

Criterion must be declared explicitly and preserved as an external reference vector.

Trajectory continuity alone is insufficient to preserve objective continuity.

Long-horizon operation therefore requires:

- persistent objective conditioning
- objective vector supervision
- trajectory monitoring
- explicit criterion transition rules


### Experimental implication

Trajectory supervision alone is sufficient for short contextual coherence.

Project supervision requires persistent objective conditioning in addition to semantic trajectory monitoring.
