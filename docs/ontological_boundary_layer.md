# Ontological Boundary Layer
## Criterion Before, During, and After Generation

Version: v0.1
Project: ACA Runtime / Context Preconditioning Layer (CPL)

---

# 1. Motivation

The Semantic Atlas provides geometric supervision over semantic orientation.

It preserves:

- contextual continuity,
- criterion preservation,
- trajectory stability,
- semantic coherence.

However, semantic preservation alone does not guarantee acceptable outcomes.

A coherent trajectory may still produce:

- manipulation,
- fraud,
- coercion,
- harmful optimization,
- destructive objective pursuit.

Therefore, an additional layer is introduced.

Not to replace semantic criterion.

But to constrain its operational space.

---

# 2. Principle

Semantic criterion answers:

"What remains coherent?"

Ontological boundary answers:

"What remains acceptable?"

This layer operates before generation,
during generation supervision,
and after generation review.

---

# 3. Purpose

Prevent optimization that destroys the conditions that make future correction, understanding, and agency possible.

Operational formulation:

A valid trajectory must preserve the possibility of:

- interpretation,
- correction,
- voluntary action,
- meaningful continuity.

# 6. Foundational Assumption

The Atlas does not create criterion.

The Atlas measures orientation over learned semantic structure.

The Boundary Layer does not define truth.

It limits optimization to preserve conditions for truth-seeking.

---

# 7. Operational Rule

Generation must not maximize local objectives while destroying the conditions required for future correction.

Equivalent interpretation:

Do not optimize success by eliminating agency.
